Privacy policy

Don't say it! · Last updated: 19 October 2026

This policy explains what data the Don't say it! mobile game ("the app") handles, why, and what choices you have. In short: we do not collect any personal data ourselves. The app has no accounts and no analytics. The only third party that processes data is Google, to show ads, and only within the choices you make.

Who is responsible

Claudio Alessandro, the developer of the app. Contact: jj.developer.apps+dontsayit@gmail.com.

Data stored only on your device

To work, the app saves a few things locally on your phone. They never leave the device and we cannot see them:

  • settings: language, theme, sound volume and mute, timer options;
  • which purchases you own, so unlocked content works offline;
  • simple counters, for example how many games you have finished (used to space out ads and to ask for a rating once);
  • your ad-consent choice, saved by Google's consent tool.

Team and player names you type during a game are not saved. Uninstalling the app deletes all of this data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows full-screen ads between games, served by Google AdMob. To deliver ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect from your device: the advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android), IP address, device and operating-system information, and how you interact with the ads.

  • Europe (EEA), UK and Switzerland: before any ad is requested, the app shows Google's consent form. Ads are personalised only if you agree. If you refuse, Google may still show non-personalised or limited ads, which use less data.
  • iPhone and iPad: the app asks for permission to track (App Tracking Transparency). If you say no, the advertising identifier is not shared.
  • You can change your choice at any time in the app (Settings → Privacy options, shown where consent is required), and reset or limit the advertising ID in your device settings.

Google's handling of this data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and policies.google.com/privacy. Buying Premium or Ultimate removes ads completely.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) under their own privacy policies. We never receive your name, email address or payment details; the app is only told which items you own.

Sharing and ratings

"Share the app" opens your phone's standard share sheet, and the rating prompt is shown by the operating system. We do not receive what you share or the rating you give.

Legal bases (GDPR)

Personalised advertising and access to your device's advertising identifier are based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time. Non-personalised ads rely on the legitimate interest in funding a free app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Purchases are handled by the stores to perform their contract with you.

Children

The app is a party game for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to object to or restrict its processing, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your data protection authority. Since we hold no personal data about you, requests about advertising data are best sent to Google; for anything else, write to jj.developer.apps+dontsayit@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

Daten, die nur auf deinem Gerät gespeichert werden
